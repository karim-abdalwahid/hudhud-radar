## 🟦 Facebook Pages

### pages_show_list
```
Hudhud is a SaaS platform where business clients connect their own Facebook Pages to our AI assistant. We use pages_show_list to display the list of Pages the logged-in user administers, so they can select which of their own Pages to connect during onboarding. Users only ever see and connect their own Pages.
```

### pages_manage_metadata
```
When a business client connects their Facebook Page, Hudhud subscribes that Page to our webhooks so we can receive real-time message and comment events. This allows our AI assistant to reply to the client's customers within Meta's 24-hour messaging window. The subscription is removed when the client disconnects their Page.
```

### pages_utility_messaging
```
Hudhud uses utility messaging to send non-promotional, service-related follow-ups to customers who interacted with the business client's Page — such as confirming that requested information was sent, or notifying them that their inquiry was received. All messages are sent within Meta's 24-hour messaging window and only to users who messaged the Page first.
```

### pages_messaging
```
Hudhud's AI assistant automatically replies to customer messages received on the business client's connected Facebook Page. This lets small businesses respond to product questions and inquiries instantly, even outside business hours. Replies are strictly within Meta's 24-hour messaging window, and the business owner can pause AI replies at any time via Human Takeover.
```

### business_management
```
Hudhud reads basic business metadata (business name) of the client's connected assets to display the correct Page and account names in their dashboard. We do not modify business settings or manage assets on behalf of the business.
```

### pages_read_engagement
```
Hudhud reads engagement metrics (reach, followers, post engagement) from the client's own connected Facebook Page to display performance analytics — growth trends, reach charts, and engagement reports — in the client's private dashboard. This data is never shared with third parties.
```

### pages_read_user_content
```
Hudhud reads comments on the business client's own posts and reels to: (1) analyze customer questions and recurring needs for the client's knowledge base, and (2) enable automated comment responses per the client's configured rules. We only read content on the client's own connected Pages.
```

### pages_manage_posts
```
Business clients use Hudhud's AI content studio to draft, schedule, and publish posts to their own connected Facebook Page. We publish on their behalf at the time they scheduled. Clients can view, edit, or delete scheduled posts in their dashboard.
```

### pages_manage_ads
```
Hudhud reads basic ad campaign performance data (spend, reach, impressions) from the client's own ad account to display ROI analytics in their dashboard. We do not create, modify, or spend on ad campaigns.
```

### pages_manage_engagement
```
Hudhud likes and replies to comments on the business client's own Facebook posts, per the client's configured automation rules (e.g., "like every comment containing the keyword 'price'"). All engagement targets only the client's own content.
```

### facebook_branded_content_ads_brand
```
Hudhud does not actively use branded content ads permissions at this stage. This permission was included in the initial app configuration during development and is not exercised by our platform's current feature set.
```

### facebook_creator_marketplace_discovery

Hudhud does not use creator marketplace discovery. This permission was included in the initial app configuration and is not exercised by our platform.
```

### read_insights
```
Hudhud reads page-level insights for the business client's own connected Facebook Page to display performance analytics (views, reach, follower growth) in their dashboard. Data is shown only to the client who connected the Page.
```

### ads_read
```
Hudhud reads campaign performance data (spend, reach, impressions, clicks) from the client's own ad account to display marketing ROI analytics in their dashboard. Read-only — we never create, modify, or pause campaigns.
```

### leads_retrieval
```
Hudhud retrieves leads generated by the business client's own Facebook Lead Ads so they can view and manage them in the client's CRM dashboard. Leads belong to the client's own ad campaigns and are stored only for the client who created them.
```

### public_profile
```
Hudhud reads the logged-in user's public profile (name, profile picture) to display their identity in the platform's sidebar and to personalize their workspace. This is standard authentication data via Facebook Login.
```

### email
```
Hudhud uses the user's email address as their unique account identifier for login, account recovery, and service notifications (e.g., trial ending, subscription confirmation). We never send marketing emails without opt-in consent, and we never share the email with third parties.
```

### Instagram Public Content Access
```
Hudhud does not use Instagram Public Content Access for hashtag search or public content scraping. This permission was included in the initial app configuration and is not exercised by our platform.
```
> **شيلها** لو متقدرش تبررها.

### Human Agent
```
Hudhud's Human Agent permission allows the business owner to manually send messages to customers who interacted with their connected Page, outside the standard 24-hour window, when the AI agent is paused and the owner personally takes over the conversation. Messages are sent only by the verified business owner to customers who initiated contact.
```

### Marketing API Access Tier
```
Hudhud does not currently use the Marketing API for ad creation or management at scale. This access tier was included during development. If granted, it will only be used for reading the client's own campaign performance data (read-only).
```

### Live Video API
```
Hudhud does not use the Live Video API. This permission was included in the initial app configuration and is not exercised by our platform.
```
> **شيلها**.

### Business Asset User Profile Access
```
Hudhud reads the business client's own profile information (Page name, profile image) for display in their dashboard so they can verify which Page is connected. This data is shown only to the client who connected the asset.
```

### Page Mentions
```
Hudhud reads mentions of the business client's own Facebook Page so the client can see and respond to customer mentions in their unified inbox. We do not post mentions or manage mention settings.
```

## 📊 إجابة أسئلة App Review الإضافية (نفس الصفحة)

### "How will your business use Platform Data?"
```
Hudhud is a SaaS platform for social selling. Business clients connect their own Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts to our platform. We use Platform Data only to deliver the service the client connected for:

1) Our AI automatically answers customer comments and direct messages on the client's own Pages and accounts, strictly within Meta's 24-hour messaging window.
2) When a customer voluntarily shares contact details (phone or email) in a chat, we store them so the business can follow up on the inquiry.
3) The client schedules and publishes content to their own Pages.
4) Each client sees statistics about the performance of their own accounts.

Every client can only access data from the accounts they connected themselves. We never sell or share Platform Data with third parties, and clients can disconnect their accounts or request data deletion at any time.
```

```
No
